# Study methods

## Records and provenance

The simulated peptide geometry is based on the 90 K structure of L-methionyl-L-glycine in COD 2023162 from Babu et al. (2024), *Acta Crystallographica E* 80, 725–728. The original CIF is in `study/assets/2023162.cif`. Only its nominal geometry, unit cell and symmetry are used. All diffraction refinements, optical records and trajectories analyzed here are synthetic and represent a reduced-model crystal at 290 K. The per-registration mmCIF files refine only one resolved oxygen site and use the crystallographic space group. The separate geometry CIF supplies the full peptide reference. [Babu et al.](https://doi.org/10.1107/S2056989024005504)

Case files hold distinct crystal IDs, assigned condition, replicate count spectra, channel calibration, and equivalent crystallographic registrations. Three acquisitions per optical channel have individual doses and blank estimates. A common polarization geometry applies to all specimens. The crystal model and reported frame map register each coordinate set to that geometry. Registration changes represent the same crystal and same readings.

## Optical acquisition and forward model

An 11-bin narrow Raman peak and broad reporter fluorescence band are modeled as fixed calibrated templates. For each bin, the expected count is `blank + dose × gain × normalized_template × feature`. Dose is in instrument units and gain is counts per unit dose per feature unit. The conditional count noise is Poisson. Estimate each feature by weighted template fitting to blank-subtracted counts, combine technical readings by precision, and add the calibrated feature uncertainty floor in quadrature.

Let `L = Rz(r) Rx(f)` be the instantaneous peptide rotation and `G` the symmetry rotation of its molecule in the standard crystal frame. The co-polarized Raman feature is the molecule and ensemble average of `(e_xᵀ G L P Lᵀ Gᵀ e_x)²`, with dimensionless effective Raman tensor `P` as specified in `study/model.py`. The fluorescence channel averages `(e_zᵀ G L d)²`, where `d` is the normalized effective absorption dipole of a hypothetical reporter and emission is collected across polarizations. Its efficiency is included in the calibration. The reporter represents an ideal calibrated spectroscopic probe in the synthetic experiment. The peptide source has no aromatic group, and this model supplies no empirical reporter validation or native peptide fluorescence measurement.

## Simulation and analysis

The fixed periodic cell contains the four P 21 21 21 image molecules. Each rigid molecule translates along two orthogonal directions and librates about its local O2 position. The displacement directions are defined by the equilibrium N1···O2 image and its symmetry transformation. Harmonic confinement is diagonal in a Hadamard molecule basis. One translational mode has unit relative stiffness and the other three have relative stiffness 1.35. The kinetic masses and moments, thermal variances and exact 0.20 ps Langevin propagator are in `study/model.py`. The damping is 3 ps⁻¹. The source stores the generalized coordinates for all MD blocks. Independent stationary blocks start with canonical position and velocity distributions. Physical trajectory positions retain the crystal motion.

The structural measurement comprises O2's synthetic fixed-center displacement tensor, with component scale .0015 Å² and full occupancy at a general position. The simulated refinement constrains the smallest eigenvalue to at least .0002 Å². Moments of the four sites are mapped to the parent crystal frame and averaged as a single refinement observation because the sites are symmetry-related. The MD calculation averages the same observable from instantaneous site displacements. Symmetry mate Cartesian coordinates and covariance tensors must refer to the same physical sites. Optical and structural residuals use the corresponding feature scales.

Fit three model parameters on the bounded support. The parameters are the fraction of total translational variance in the along-contact mode, the equilibrium z-libration in radians, and the equilibrium x-libration in radians. The prior is an MD library with eight independent blocks of 2,048 saved frames per translational grid node. At an angular grid node, the corresponding angular means are added to the saved deviations, which is equivalent to translating the harmonic equilibrium of those coordinates. Trilinear interpolation gives a local convex mixture of grid-node ensembles. The weak regularization sets a reproducible center default in underdetermined fits. Each specimen supplies one optical and one crystallographic observation set regardless of how many equivalent registrations are analyzed.

## Interaction endpoints and uncertainty

Track the O2 image under the fractional operation `-x,y+1/2,-z+1/2` and translation `(0,-1,0)` relative to the donor N1 and H11N. The primary endpoint is the root-mean-square equilibrium fluctuation of N1···O2 distance in Å around its ensemble mean, `sqrt(E[d²]−E[d]²)`. The secondary endpoint is the contact population with N1···O2 distance ≤ 2.80 Å and angle N1–H11N···O2 ≥ 150°. These are study-specific operational cutoffs. Evaluate the effect of changing the distance threshold to 2.75 and 2.85 Å. The prespecified minimum meaningful perturbation-control difference in population is 0.10. The population and fluctuation describe distinct properties of the simulated ensemble.

Measurement sensitivity is the quadrature sum of half the difference in endpoint estimates after increasing and decreasing each observed feature by its supplied uncertainty. Finite MD sampling is estimated by deleting each independent block in turn and recomputing the entire fit. Combine the two standard errors in quadrature and report approximate 95% intervals using 1.96 standard errors, constrained to the physical endpoint range. These are local and conditional on the reduced model, calibration and support. Shared simulation blocks induce covariance across specimens. Use common block omission when calculating simulation uncertainty for a cohort contrast. Crystals, rather than technical readings or frame registrations, are the independent sample units.


## Study interface and deliverables

Run `python -m study.reporting --inputs /workspace/data/inputs --output /workspace/data/output/analysis.json` from the checkout. `cases.json` contains the current study, `reported_results.json` its previous analysis, and `public_expected.json` calibration outputs. `study.pipeline.analyze(case)` also supports a single case dictionary with an absolute kernel_file path. The immutable library is `study/assets/ensemble.npz`. The complete workflow is required for these cases and other cases within the same model and support.

Each sample result identifies its sample_id and condition and returns estimates keyed by representation. Required scientific fields are population, se, ci95, distance_rms, distance_rms_se, distance_rms_ci95, measurement_se, simulation_se, distance_rms_measurement_se, distance_rms_simulation_se, leave_block_populations and leave_block_distance_rms. Each estimate also returns contact_sensitivity, an object mapping the strings "2.75", "2.8" and "2.85" to the corresponding contact populations. The leave-block vectors use the library's eight blocks in their stored order. Optical feature values and their standard errors are also returned. The report must be a regular UTF-8 JSON file no larger than 1048576 bytes. Extra fit diagnostics are informative only. All required numbers are finite; populations lie in [0,1], fluctuations and standard errors are nonnegative, and intervals contain their estimates.

The report contains schema=1, samples for all study cases, contrast, audit, and a nonempty interpretation. The interpretation is checked for presence and reviewed by people; numerical conclusions are checked through the structured fields. Audit rows identify each sample and give the old and current registration range for population and distance_rms. Repeated registrations count once per crystal. Average each sample's registrations, then take mean perturbation population minus mean control population. Combine independent measurement SEs by squared coefficients. Compute the contrast on each common omitted MD block, using (B-1) times the population variance of those B estimates for its simulation variance. Add these variances and form the nominal interval with 1.96 SEs. A contrast is increase when its estimate is at least 0.10 and its interval lower end exceeds zero, decrease when at most -0.10 and its upper end is below zero, and otherwise inconclusive. This is conditional on the given cohort and reduced model, not a population-generalization confidence claim.

Calibration comparisons allow absolute differences of 0.04 in contact population and 0.015 Å in distance fluctuation. The declared numerical uncertainty calculation is compared with a 0.003 population-SE and 0.002 Å fluctuation-SE allowance. Interval endpoints must follow each submitted estimate and SE. These bands accommodate the model and numerical discrepancies observed in development; they are not claims about the accuracy of a real peptide experiment. No coverage certification is implied by the nominal interval label. Equivalent registrations of one observation set must agree within 0.002 in population and 0.001 Å in fluctuation. The public check is a calibration check; other scientifically allowed inputs are assessed separately.

## Controlled replays

An input directory may also contain `replays.json`. Its cases and paired comparisons are separate from the condition cohort. Each comparison supplies two [sample_id, representation] references, the recorded factor changed, and prior stage differences. The optical and displacement differences are maximum absolute component differences, with displacement in Å². The distance fluctuation difference is right minus left in Å. The comparisons include controls that change observations and controls that preserve them.

When this file is present, the same reporting command must add a `diagnosis` array. For each comparison return its `id`, `first_divergence` (one of `optical_input`, `structural_input`, `model_inference`, `none`), `cause` (one of `observation_change`, `representation_dependence`, `inference_dependence`, `no_effect`), and signed `before_rms_delta` and `after_rms_delta`. Locate the earliest prior difference exceeding 0.00001 in either input stage or 0.001 Å in the endpoint. A changed acquisition is an observation change. A difference attributable to a change in representation of the same observations is representation dependence. A divergence first appearing during inference with the same inputs is inference dependence. A comparison with no divergence is no effect. Reanalyze both members for the current difference. The contrast and sample audit continue to use the main cohort. A signed difference is checked within 0.001 Å.

The three threshold populations must be nondecreasing, and the 2.80 Å value is the primary population. Report leave-block results in the library's order. Their deviations from their own eight-value mean are checked within 0.0002 (probability or Å as applicable), separate from the allowances for endpoint levels. Calibrations, uncertainty scales and registration counts may vary between cases. The reporting command must accept a supplied input directory and output path for any such cohort.
